# Northwind Handbook (sample corpus)

## Holiday
Staff receive 25 days of paid holiday each year, plus public holidays.
Unused days do not carry into the next year.

## Expenses
Expenses under 50 are approved by a line manager. Anything above 50 needs
finance approval before the money is spent, not after.

## Equipment
Laptops are replaced every three years. A broken laptop is replaced within two
working days, and the old device is wiped before it leaves the building.
